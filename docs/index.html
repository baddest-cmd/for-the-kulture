CHRONOLOGICAL JOURNEY MATRIX

01

Catalog Seeding & Onboarding

Persona: Highly Habitual power-user and researcher.

Phase Goal: Bypass the algorithmic "cold start" recommendation loop by migrating a massive historical music catalog.

+4 (High Anticipation)

Pain Points: Time-consuming manual configuration, but fueled by positive expectations.

"Got excited after importing my Apple music catalog thinking I would at least not have to do the heavy lifting."

BE Lens (Optimism Bias): Overestimating the platform's ability to seamlessly ingest and align recommended tracks with historical data.

Fix: Introduce inline progress transparency and provide an immediate confirmation check explaining how the imported catalog is mapped.
02

Device Connection & Playback Initialisation

Persona: Active Focus Listener.

Phase Goal: Establish a stable wireless pairing with external physical speakers and start playback smoothly.

-2 (Frustrated)

Pain Points: The interface stalls on a circling loader icon, signaling that wireless pairing is automated, when in fact it requires the user to manually click the targeted device.

"A circling icon led them to believe the connection was in progress, but they actually needed to click on the device."

BE Lens (Mental Model Violation): Design pattern masks an active choice as an automated system state.

Fix: Replace the ambiguous circling icon with high-contrast text or a pulsing button indicator.
03

Algorithmic Feed Failure

Persona: Cultural Music Consumer.

Phase Goal: Navigate the generated home feed to discover relevant music that fits established tastes.

-5 (Trust Broken)

Pain Points: Total erasure of imported history. The home feed is flooded with generic local defaults—specifically Gospel music.

"Gospel is popular in South Africa but I will be honest I have never in my life played a gospel song. This disappointed me."

BE Lens (Socioeconomic Incongruence): Applying lazy, sweeping macro-regional defaults over explicit micro-user preferences.

Fix: Implement strict fallback guardrails that weight direct user-seeded library history at 80%.
04

Focus Work & Playback Loop Gaps

Persona: Deep Focus Worker.

Phase Goal: Loop curated focus playlists (such as airplane mode) to block out background noise during work sessions.

-3 (Distracted)

Pain Points: Playlists on repeat halt completely after the last track instead of looping. Unrelated content is forcefully appended via intrusive autoplay.

"Auto-play maybe off but respect my input repeat the playlist tho ????"

BE Lens (Habit Disruption): Intrusive, automated media loading shifts the user from a state of low-effort focus back to a high-effort state.

Fix: Fix the repetition system so repeat strictly confines playback to the user's explicit playlist.
05

Cross-Platform Playlist Parity

Persona: Mobile-first Commuter (South African Market Context).

Phase Goal: Stream or sync custom playlists for uninterrupted offline listening on mobile.

-4 (Restricted)

Pain Points: Severe parity gap—the web app displays and plays all 24 tracks, whereas the mobile app restricts the exact same playlist to only about 8 songs.

"mobile app experience is the most restricting currently... for the South African base B2C products need to mobile app first"

BE Lens (Economic Detachment): Failing to optimize for a "mobile-first" South African consumer base experiencing extraordinarily high mobile data tariffs.

Fix: Standardize core playlist syncing architecture across platforms.
06

The Vision for Proactive Delight

Persona: Highly Engaged Habitual Listener.

Phase Goal: Stay effortlessly updated on new releases from favorite artists and immediately resume playback.

+5 (Ultimate Delight)

Delight Points: Context-aware, highly direct homescreen notifications and the lock screen resume banner.

"proactive music experience... esp for highly habitual listeners this is fire"

BE Lens (Salience & Choice Architecture): Reducing the physical and cognitive path to action to absolute zero.

Fix: Transition from a passive feed audit layout to proactive, highly tailored opt-in notifications.
